My Two Years at Cuesta College

I just felt like this song represents the quick-paced lifestyle I lived while in SLO

A panoramic view of San Luis Obispo

After growing up in Lamorinda, attending Cuesta College introduced me to a new environment and a different stage of my life. Over the course of two years, I became more independent, learned how to manage the responsibilities of college, and gained a clearer understanding of myself and the direction I wanted to take. I found a more profound love for the ocean and animals as the it was always the place I floated to when I was stressed and needed time alone.

My First Year

My first year at Cuesta College was a period of adjustment. I had to become comfortable in a new setting while learning how to organize my schedule, complete college-level work, and take greater responsibility for my education. I took my first year there as an opportunity to discover other interests I otherwise would not have encountered in Lamorinda. I also was able to meet a lot of my closest friends I have today!

My Second Year

During my second year, I felt more comfortable with college life and more confident in my abilities. I developed stronger routines, improved the way I managed my time, and began thinking more seriously about my future. Of course, I have to continue locking in so that I could transfer into my 4 year (SJSU). I expanded my skills by becoming an employee at the college as a marketing assistant where I learned more about graphic design, CMS, photography, videography, and obviously, marketing.
