I grew up in Lamorinda, a beautiful area in Contra Costa County made up of Lafayette, Moraga, and Orinda. Surrounded by rolling hills, parks, and hiking trails, it was a peaceful and welcoming place to call home. Growing up there gave me the opportunity to enjoy nature while still being close to Oakland, Berkeley, and the rest of the Bay Area. Lamorinda will always be special to me because of its close-knit community and the memories I made there.

I wonder where this takes me...

During my high school years, I was involved in tennis and several school clubs, which gave me opportunities to meet new people, work as part of a team, and become more connected to my school community. Playing tennis taught me the importance of patience, practice, and supporting my teammates. Outside of school, I enjoyed exploring Lamorinda’s rolling hills with my camera and photographing the area’s scenery. Taking pictures allowed me to be creative while noticing details in nature that I might otherwise have missed. I also liked visiting Walnut Creek Plaza and spending time with my friends. One thing I especially appreciated about Lamorinda was that it always felt like a safe and peaceful place to explore, enjoy nature, and make lasting memories.
