Are You Sure?

Play the game

You've been paying $14.99 a month for a streaming service you never use. Signing up took one click. Now you just want to cancel... but the website has other plans.

How to play: chase the real cancel button and click it. It runs away from your mouse. Don't click the big, bright buttons. They're traps, and clicking one restarts the level. Get through all 5 levels as fast as you can and try to beat your best time.

About the game: this started as the goose and carrot chase game from class. I kept the chase, but turned the carrot into a cancel button. Each level is based on a real "dark pattern," a design trick websites use to make cancelling hard: endless "Are you sure?" screens, a "Cancel" button that cancels your cancellation, and a green button that looks like the right choice but isn't. Every button is the opposite of good UX, so you can feel how frustrating it is. And when you finally win, the game asks if you want to Pay Again.
