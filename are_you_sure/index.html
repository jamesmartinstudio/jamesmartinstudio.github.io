Are You Sure?

You're paying $14.99 a month for a streaming service you never use. Signing up took one click. Now you want to cancel, but the website has other plans.

How to play

  • Chase the real cancel button and click it.
  • Avoid the bright decoy buttons. Clicking one restarts the level.
  • Get through all five levels and try to beat your best time.

A game about the design tricks that make cancelling harder than it should be.

By James Martin
