About Me

I’m studying Digital Media Art at San José State University and am currently the product designer at Gradient Labs, where I work across product, branding, and growth to shape a new approach to prediction markets.

I’m most interested in products shaped by culture, competition, and discovery—the kinds of experiences that become part of people’s routines rather than simply helping them complete a task.

When I’m not designing, I’m listening to music, playing tennis, doing portrait photography, graphic design, improving my Spanish speaking skills, or exploring ideas that eventually find their way back into my work.
